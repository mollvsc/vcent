Ahoy!

I want to talk a bit about a tiny personal project I've wanted to pursue for a while. It has a fairly comprehensible name - OneMinuteVlogs. It combines my newfound passion of videography with my earnest attempt at showcasing content to the attention-deficit crowd. So, before I lose you (again), I aim to create one minute video diaries, basically videos that are consumable at ease without wasting peoples' time. It'll mostly be of places I visit or events I find interesting. Hopefully, I'll be able to string together something worth watching. What suits this OneMinuteVlogs project even more is that it's Instagram-friendly since they've now increased the video length limit to one minute. 

The first video in the OneMinuteVlogs series is from a recent trip to Singapore. The city appealed to me from the get-go. It is modern, organized, progressive and cosmopolitan. The video doesn't capture everything about Singapore but it showcases some of my experiences in those 4 days. The places shown in the video are - the Marina Bay, Universal Studios, Arab Street & Haji Lane, Club Zouk, Din Tai Fung and some of the areas around Scotts Road. 

Apologies for the jerky video footage. My YI action camera doesn't do too well with video stabilization but I shall attempt to rectify it by using a mount next time instead of holding it with my hands.
